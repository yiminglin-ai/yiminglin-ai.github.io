Yiming Lin

I am a Research Scientist at Meta Reality Labs, working on generative AI for real-time avatars and video. I help turn research ideas into products at scale; recent work includes AI-powered video translation and lip sync, creator embodiment, and real-time holograms.

Previously, I led research and development in face perception, AI fairness, and responsible AI at Realeyes, and worked on face analysis, emotion recognition, and virtual avatars at Meta AI (formerly Facebook AI).

I completed my PhD at Imperial College London under Professor Maja Pantic and remain an affiliated honorary research fellow with the iBUG group.
